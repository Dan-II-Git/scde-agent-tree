Foundations

Design Principles

Four principles shape every screen, token, and component in Look Deeper.

Clarity before cleverness

Financial data should read quickly. Favor tabular numerals, generous whitespace, and a muted palette — reserve color for meaning.

Rooted in SCDE

The palmetto palette — navy, gold, slate — carries across every surface. Gold is an accent, not a wallpaper.

Honest hierarchy

One headline per view. Numbers earn the largest type. Supporting detail recedes so the story leads.

Accessible by default

All text-on-background pairs meet WCAG AA. Focus states are visible, touch targets are 40px+, and color is never the only signal.

Usage

Do & don't

Shorthand rules for applying the system. When in doubt, defer to the SCDE Brand Guidelines.

Do

Lead with navy surfaces for nav and heavy chrome; keep content areas on light ink-50.

Use gold sparingly — accent bars on KPI cards, the primary brand mark, hero eyebrows.

Pair numbers with tabular-nums and right-alignment in tables.

Maintain 4px baseline rhythm — snap to the spacing scale.

Don't

Don't use gold for long blocks of text or large fills — it loses the accent quality.

Don't introduce new families. Poppins for UI, JetBrains Mono for numerics.

Don't use color alone to signal state — always pair with a badge label or icon.

Don't apply gradients, glows, or decorative shadows. Shadows are subtle elevation only.
